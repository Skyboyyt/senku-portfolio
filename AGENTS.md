# Website design preferences

- The owner prefers natural, considered websites. Do not add decorative arrows to links or buttons, starbursts, circular slogan stamps, or repetitive slogan strips.
- Use clear text labels for actions. Navigation controls may use conventional hamburger, close, sun and moon icons when they communicate function.
- Provide responsive navigation and accessible dark/light mode for website work unless the owner specifies otherwise.
- Prefer relevant real photography with a recorded source and suitable reuse terms. Download and optimize project imagery rather than relying on remote hotlinks.
- Preserve the portfolio design restored on 22 October 2026; additions should fit its existing layout.
- Label fictional businesses and simulated actions honestly. Do not claim frontend demos create real appointments, emails or payments.
