# Website design preferences

- The owner prefers natural, considered websites. Do not add decorative arrows to links or buttons, or circular slogan stamps.
- The "Ideas in motion" design (25 October 2026) includes two owner-approved motifs: the rotating starburst in the hero and contact scenes, and the scrolling slogan strips (Design / Build / Interact and similar). Keep them; don't add more of either without asking.
- Use clear text labels for actions. Navigation controls may use conventional hamburger, close, sun and moon icons when they communicate function.
- Provide responsive navigation and accessible dark/light mode for website work unless the owner specifies otherwise.
- Prefer relevant real photography and real product screenshots with a recorded source and suitable reuse terms. Download and optimize project imagery rather than relying on remote hotlinks.
- Preserve the "Ideas in motion" portfolio design introduced on 25 October 2026 (it replaced the design restored on 22 October 2026); additions should fit its scene layout, palette, type and motion.
- Label fictional businesses and simulated actions honestly. Do not claim frontend demos create real appointments, emails or payments.
