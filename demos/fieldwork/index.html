BEFORE YOU TAKE A SEAT

How does the booking demo work?

Pick a service, barber, day and sample time, then enter a first name and email to preview the confirmation. Use made-up details. Nothing is sent, and no appointment is created with a real business.

Is my information saved?

No. Form details stay in this page's memory, are cleared when the booking window closes, and disappear on refresh. This demo has no booking database or email service.

Could this work for my business?

Yes. This concept can be adapted to a salon, studio or another appointment-based business. A live version would need your services, real availability, a booking backend and any email or payment integrations you choose. Talk to Fahad about your project.
